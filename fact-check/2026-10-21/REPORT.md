## Method and confidence

- **ABA data** was compared programmatically against spreadsheets downloaded from the ABA's own backend (`backend.abarequireddisclosures.org`) today. This is high confidence. The same checks now live in the repo: `tools/fetch_aba.py --dry-run` reports any field that differs from the ABA.
- **BLS and MERIC** were compared programmatically against the BLS API and archived MERIC tables. High confidence.
- **FBI 2024** rates came from secondary sources (Wikipedia and USAFacts), because the primary table couldn't be fetched. Medium-high confidence.
- **US News ranks (current edition)** were checked directly against usnews.com, loaded in Chrome on 2026-10-21. That page lists 194 schools: 174 with a numeric rank and 20 in a "#175–194" unranked band, plus Jacksonville listed separately as "Unranked". Every school on the site was matched to its usnews.com entry. The TaxProf-based table in `details/us-news-ranks.md` agreed on 192 of 193; the one miss was **Loyola Chicago (#70, not #77)**, now corrected there. Wilmington/Farnan doesn't appear on usnews.com at all, which is consistent with its provisional ABA status. High confidence.
- **US News ranks (2025–26, what the site shows today)** can't be checked on usnews.com anymore, since it only shows the current edition. Those come from TaxProf Blog's reposted list, cross-checked against a second TaxProf table. Medium-high confidence.
- **Writeup and blurb prose** was checked by web research against school sites, US News specialty ranks and ABA bar data. Each flagged item cites its source in `details/`.
- **Not checked:** the editorial 1–5 scores (livability, city social/outdoors/walkability). They're opinion by design. Nothing was flagged as absurd.
