## Claims checked and confirmed (selected)
- Ada, OH, "about 5,000": 5,334 (2020 census; 5,302 in the 2023 estimate). OK.
- Grundy, VA, "about 1,000": 875 (2020 census), -12.5%, within tolerance. Optional tweak: "under 1,000".
- South Royalton, VT, "a few hundred": 654 (2020 CDP). OK.
- Vermillion, SD, "about 11,000": 11,695 (2020). OK.
- Laramie, "7,200 feet": ~7,165 ft. OK.
- Transit claims all check out: PATCO (Camden), Metro-North (White Plains), LIRR (Hempstead), Green Line light rail (St. Paul), Valley Metro light rail (Tempe), the T (Newton), SEPTA regional rail (Villanova), Newark to Manhattan in ~20 min, Wilmington to Philly in ~30 min on Amtrak.
- Distance claims I'd leave alone: Akron to Cleveland, Norman to OKC, Topeka to KC, Salem to Portland, Toledo to Detroit (~55-60 min, borderline "under an hour"), South Bend to Lake Michigan, Spokane to skiing (Mt. Spokane), Sacramento to Tahoe.
- SLC "seven ski resorts within an hour" is conservative (sources commonly say 10+). OK.

## Egregious score contradictions
None found. All 147 score sets are within reasonable editorial range. The nearest to borderline is Davis (social 2), and the Sacramento proximity issue sits in the blurb, not the score.

## Clean count
**138 of 147 blurbs checked out clean.** 9 blurbs have flagged claims (Hartford, Tucson, Davis, Tempe, Fairfax, Ave Maria x2, Denver, Oklahoma City, Laramie). Of those, Tempe and Fairfax are law-school location issues rather than false statements in the blurb.
