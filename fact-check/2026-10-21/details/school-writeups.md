## Checked out clean

These writeups' concrete claims check out, and anything left over is opinion.

- **California:** UCLA, UC Berkeley (Berkeley ties Stanford for #1 in IP; resident tuition $62.5k vs $76.1k nonresident, so "modest discount" holds), USC, UC Irvine, UC Davis (environmental law #18), Pepperdine (dispute resolution #3 in 2026-27, #2 in 2025-26, so "nationally ranked" is correct), San Diego, Loyola LA, UC Law SF (class of about 374 is large; Civic Center location), Southwestern (64.6% bar vs 77.6% state, so "lags" is right), Santa Clara (IP #4), USF, California Western (60.8% vs 77.6%, so "below-median" is right).
- **Florida:** Florida State, FIU ("perennial #1 or #2" holds: #1 in Florida in the ABA 2025 file at 88.89%, ahead of UF at 87.71%), Miami, Stetson (trial advocacy #1 in both 2025-26 and 2026-27), Barry, Nova Southeastern (68.3% vs 72.4%), St. Thomas (81.6% vs 72.4% state; "improving" understates it, since it is now 5th in Florida). FAMU's "lowest in-state cost in Florida" is also correct ($13,680 vs $19-21k at UF, FSU and FIU).
- **Georgia:** UGA, Georgia State, Mercer (legal writing #16, and trial advocacy has risen to #6, which the writeup could mention), Atlanta's John Marshall (66.7% vs 79.9%).
- **Illinois:** Northwestern (67.4% Big Law vs Chicago's 59.7% in the site's data; heavy work-experience preference is well documented), Illinois, DePaul, Northern Illinois, Southern Illinois.
- **Maryland:** Maryland (health law #6), Baltimore.
- **Massachusetts:** Harvard, Boston College (Newton campus is correct), Northeastern (co-op is correct; narrative evaluations with no grades, GPA or class rank are confirmed on the school's degree-requirements page), Suffolk.

## Unverifiable or subjective claims worth a look

- **Miami:** "Largest alumni network in South Florida." This is plausible given its size and age, but I found no source that measures it. Nova Southeastern and St. Thomas are also described as having "large" South Florida networks. Consider "one of the largest."
- **San Diego:** "Best-connected school in the San Diego market." This is opinion. Cal Western is the same size and has deep local roots. Consider "among the best-connected."
- **Northwestern:** "strongest Big Law and corporate placement in Chicago." The site's data supports this by Big Law *percentage*, but Chicago likely matches it on Chicago-market prestige. It's defensible but arguable.
- **Florida:** "The clear value leader in Florida… strongest statewide placement" is opinion. FIU beat UF on bar passage, and FIU and FSU tuition is within $1.6k of UF's.
- **Harvard:** "it's full sticker." Like Stanford, Harvard is need-based only and has no merit aid. "Full sticker" is fair for students without demonstrated need, but "need-based aid only" is more precise.
- **Boston University:** "standout tax… programs." General tax ranks #17, but the graduate tax program ranks #8, so the claim holds if it means the LLM program.
- **Pepperdine:** "improving outcomes." **Northern Illinois** and **Southern Illinois** writeups say nothing about weak bar passage (57.7% and 60.4% vs a 78.6% Illinois average). That's not wrong, but it's a notable omission given how other writeups flag bar risk.
- Pure opinion, not checked: "collegial culture" (Northwestern, Boston College), "tight alumni network" (USC), "famously rigorous" (Chicago), "commuter feel" (Georgia State).

## Checked out clean

These writeups are clean (or all their concrete claims verified): UMass Dartmouth, New England Law, Western New England, Michigan, Wayne State, Michigan State, Detroit Mercy, NYU, Columbia, Cardozo, Buffalo, Syracuse, Albany, Brooklyn, New York Law School, Hofstra, Pace, CUNY, Touro, Duke, UNC, Wake Forest, Campbell, South Carolina, Charleston, Tennessee, Belmont, Memphis, Lincoln Memorial, Texas A&M, SMU, Houston, Texas Tech, South Texas, St. Mary's, UNT Dallas.

The notable verifications:
- **UMass Dartmouth:** It is the only public law school in MA. It is "by far the cheapest": $31,766 in-state, with WNE next at $50,320.
- **Michigan State:** It fully integrated into MSU in Aug 2020 ([MSU Today](https://msutoday.msu.edu/news/2020/college-of-law-completes-full-integration)).
- **Detroit Mercy:** The dual US/Canadian JD with Windsor is real. "Unique" matches the school's own claim ([Windsor](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/canadian-american-dual-juris-doctor/)).
- **Pace:** It is #1 in environmental law in both the USN 2026-27 and 2025-26 lists ([TaxProf](https://taxprofblog.aals.org/2026/04/15/2026-27-u-s-news-environmental-law-rankings/)).
- **Cardozo:** It is #4 in dispute resolution and tied #7 in IP in USN 2026-27 ([TaxProf](https://taxprofblog.aals.org/2026/04/14/2026-27-u-s-news-dispute-resolution-rankings/)).
- **CUNY:**
  - It is #1 for public-interest placement in USN 2026 ([CUNY](https://www.law.cuny.edu/newsroom_post/cuny-law-ranked-1-in-the-nation-for-sending-graduates-into-public-interest-law/)), so "premier public-interest school" is defensible.
  - It has the lowest tuition in NYC ($15,450).
- **Hofstra / South Texas:** Both have strong advocacy programs: USN trial advocacy tied #12 and tied #6 respectively ([TaxProf](https://taxprofblog.aals.org/2026/04/20/2026-27-u-s-news-trial-advocacy-rankings/)).
- **Syracuse:** It has the Institute for Security Policy and Law plus the JDinteractive hybrid JD.
- **Charleston:** "For-profit history" is accurate. It became a 501(c)(3) on Dec 1, 2024 ([Charleston Law](https://charlestonlaw.edu/charleston-law-becomes-501c3)). Its bar passage trails USC: 70.0% vs. 85.2% in SC.
- **Texas A&M:** "Fastest riser" is backed by the school's documented climb from unranked/149 to #22 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A%26M_University_School_of_Law)), though the superlative is the school's own.
- **Cheapest-in-state claims:** NCCU ($13,444) is the lowest in-state cost in NC. UNT Dallas ($16,860) is the cheapest Texas JD.
- **St. Mary's:** Its bar rate (66.4%) trails every Texas public school.
- **Belmont / Tennessee bar:** Belmont's "solid early bar numbers" (93.9%) and Tennessee's "good bar passage" (91.3%) are both accurate. Belmont actually beat UT.

## Unverifiable / opinion claims worth a look

- **Buffalo, "The best value in New York":** This is opinion. CUNY is cheaper ($15,450 vs. $26,430), though Buffalo's bar rate is much higher (86% vs. 67%). Consider "best value among SUNY/upstate options" or "one of the best values in New York".
- **SMU, "Best-connected school in the Dallas Big Law market":** Plausible but unmeasurable. UT has the higher overall Big Law rate (39% vs. 34%), but SMU's grads are 93% in Texas. Consider "one of the best-connected...".
- **Baylor, "small-town location":** Waco has about 145k people. "Mid-size city" is more accurate.
- **UMass Dartmouth / Elon / UNT Dallas, "Newer":** UMass Law dates from 2010 and UNT Dallas from 2014, which is fair. Elon dates from 2006 (about 20 years), so "newer" is getting stretched.
- **Pure opinion, fine as-is:** Duke "famously supportive culture", Wake Forest "collegial", Cornell "close-knit", Columbia "arguably the best Wall Street placement", NYU "most expensive city", Albany "unbeatable for NY State government", Michigan State "ranking has been volatile".
